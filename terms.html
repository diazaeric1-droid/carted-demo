Carted's 2 AM Room is a small, kind place for people who are up late. To keep it that way, everyone who posts agrees to these rules. Using the room means you accept them.

There is zero tolerance for objectionable content and for abusive users. Content that breaks these rules is removed, and people who break them are blocked.

What you agree to when you post

  • No objectionable content. No hateful, harassing, threatening, sexual, or violent content; no content that targets or demeans any person or group; no spam, scams, or advertising.
  • No personal information. Don't post anyone's private information (yours or others'), including full names, addresses, phone numbers, or account details.
  • Be gentle. People come here lonely and tired. Harassment, bullying, or cruelty toward other users is not allowed.
  • You own what you post and are responsible for it. Don't post anything you don't have the right to share.

How we keep the room safe

  • Filtering. Posts are automatically screened for objectionable content before they appear.
  • Reporting. Every post has a report button (🚩). Reported content is hidden pending review and acted on promptly (typically within 24 hours).
  • Blocking. Abusive users are blocked from the room.
  • Ephemerality. Posts burn at sunrise (4 AM) — nothing is permanent, and no one is keeping score.

"Resident ghosts" are fiction

Posts labeled 👻 "resident ghost" are friendly fictional content written by us to keep the room warm. They are not real people, and this is disclosed in the room.
