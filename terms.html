👻 Carted — Community Terms & EULA

Effective: September 6, 2026 · These terms cover tonight's community table, private booths, and any user-generated content in Carted.

Carted's community table is an optional, moderated nightly check-in. People post under a persistent nickname connected to a private, automatically issued community credential; there are no random matches or direct messages. To keep it welcoming, everyone who posts agrees to these rules.

There is zero tolerance for objectionable content and for abusive users. Content that breaks these rules is removed, and people who break them are blocked.

What you agree to when you post

  • No objectionable content. No hateful, harassing, threatening, sexual, or violent content; no content that targets or demeans any person or group; no spam, scams, or advertising.
  • No personal information. Don't post anyone's private information (yours or others'), including full names, addresses, phone numbers, or account details.
  • Be gentle. Harassment, bullying, or cruelty toward other users is not allowed.
  • You own what you post and are responsible for it. Don't post anything you don't have the right to share.

How we keep the table safe

  • Filtering. Built-in server-side checks screen notes and nicknames for recognizable abuse and spam before publication. An optional external classifier adds screening when available. Automated checks are imperfect; please report content they miss.
  • Reporting. Human notes have a report control. A successfully sent report hides the note for the reporter and places it in the moderation queue; reports do not automatically remove a note for everyone. If a report cannot be sent, contact support. Carted is not an emergency response service.
  • Blocking. Abusive users are blocked from the community table.
  • Ephemerality. Ordinary notes are removed from our server within 36 hours. Reported or legally preserved material follows the exceptions described in our privacy policy.

Mort is fiction

Mort's host notes, postcards, and stories are authored fictional content. Mort is not a human participant, a live support person, or a private-message service. A Mort avatar beside a community nickname represents that person's words; it does not mean Mort wrote or endorsed the note.

Private connections and invitations

Do not share your private community connection token or attempt to claim another person's identity. Clearing app storage can create a new community identity and does not restore old friend membership. Older invitations may be read-only; create a new secure invitation to participate. A Booth is private to whoever holds its link, not a guarantee that an invited person will keep its content private.

If you're having a hard night

If a post suggests someone may be in crisis, we show a link to the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988, free, 24/7 in the US). Carted is not a crisis service, a substitute for professional help, or a medical provider.
