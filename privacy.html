👻 Carted — Privacy Policy

Effective: July 30, 2026 · previous version July 9, 2026

Carted is a simulated "window shopping" experience: you browse fictional restaurants, place orders that are never real, and keep your money. Our privacy posture matches the product: we want as little of your data as possible.

No accounts

Carted has no sign-up, no login, and no user accounts. Your streaks, favorites, progress, and your ghost's name are stored only on your device. If you delete the app, that data is gone — we never had it. (Posts you choose to share in the 2 AM Room work differently — see below.)

What we do collect

To understand whether people come back (and nothing else), the app sends anonymous usage analytics to our server:

  • a random app-generated device identifier (pseudonymous — not your Apple ID, not your advertising ID, and it cannot name you),
  • event names (e.g. "opened the app", "placed a pretend order") with timestamps,
  • a count of distinct nights the app has been opened.

That's the whole list. No name, no email, no contacts, no photos, no purchase history — there is nothing to purchase. Honesty note on the identifier: it is pseudonymous rather than perfectly anonymous — it cannot name you, but events from the same device do link together. We keep it random and app-generated precisely so it can't be joined to your Apple ID, advertising ID, or anything else.

"Near me" and your location

If you tap "Near me," your device asks for permission and then your coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap's services — Nominatim (to turn them into a neighborhood name) and Overpass (to find real places near you to fictionalize). Those services see the coordinates and your IP address, as any website you visit does; they run under the OpenStreetMap Foundation's privacy policy. We never send your location to our own server, never store it, and never attach it to analytics. Skip the feature and no location ever leaves the device.

What we never do

  • No tracking across other apps or websites; no advertising SDKs; no data brokers.
  • We never sell or rent your data, and we never share it for anyone else's purposes.
  • No third-party analytics SDKs — the beacon above is our own, hosted on Supabase (our database provider, acting as our processor).

Service providers we use

These are all of them, and what each one sees. Providers process data to run Carted, not for their own purposes (public services like OpenStreetMap operate under their own policies):

  • GitHub Pages — hosts the web version. Like any web host, it sees standard request logs (your IP address, pages loaded).
  • Supabase — our database and server host. Analytics events and room posts live here.
  • OpenAI — the text of a room post is sent to OpenAI's moderation service before it publishes, to screen for abuse and for content indicating someone may be in crisis. Only the post text is sent — never your device identifier. OpenAI does not train its models on this API content, but under its standard API policy it may retain inputs for up to ~30 days for abuse monitoring. If the check is unavailable, the post falls back to our own keyword filter.
  • OpenStreetMap services (Nominatim, Overpass) — only if you use "Near me" or search for a place; see the location section above.
  • TheMealDB — dish photos are loaded from its image host, which (like any image host) sees your IP when pictures load.

That is the complete list. Nothing else leaves your device.

Notifications

If you opt in, the app schedules a single local "last call" notification on your device at your usual hour. It's generated on-device and never fires on a night you've already played. Turn it off any time in iOS Settings.

The 2 AM Room

Posts labeled "resident ghost" are fictional content written by us. When you post in the room, your post text is sent to our server (Supabase, acting as our processor) together with the same random device identifier described above — no name, no email, nothing that identifies you — so that other people in the room can see it that night.

Before a post publishes, its text passes two screens: our own keyword filter for abuse, and OpenAI's moderation service (see above). A separate check looks for language suggesting the writer may be in crisis; if it fires, we show that writer a card with crisis-line details. We never notify anyone else, and we never act on it beyond showing that card.

Posts burn at sunrise — room posts are automatically deleted from our server within 48 hours. A copy of your own posts also lives in your device's local storage until sunrise so the room works offline.

One exception, and we want to be plain about it. If a post is reported, it is hidden immediately and held out of the sunrise burn: ordinarily up to 90 days so a human can review it. If content is of the kind the law requires us to report (in the U.S., to NCMEC's CyberTipline under 18 U.S.C. §2258A), it is preserved for as long as the law requires — currently one year after such a report — and provided to the authorities. Reported posts are the only content that outlives the sunrise burn. Nothing you write is otherwise archived, profiled, or shared.

Children

Carted is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Data retention & deletion

The anonymous analytics events carry no identity, so they cannot be tied back to you. Want the events from your device deleted anyway? Email us the request and delete the app — new events stop immediately.
