👻 Carted — Support

What is this app?

Carted is the anti-delivery app: browse midnight kitchens in 50 real cities, fill a cart with real dishes, place the order — and a ghost courier flies it across the globe and never arrives. You keep the money. One order a night; the kitchens close at 4am.

Common questions

Is anything real? The cities and the dishes are real. The restaurants are fictional, the couriers are ghosts, and no money ever changes hands. That's the whole point.

What is the community table? It's an optional asynchronous check-in: one shared question and one moderated note per person each night. Human notes use persistent nicknames and real reactions. Mort's separate nightly card is always labeled fictional.

Can I fix my nickname? Yes. Open Profile → Community nickname. Your first correction is available immediately; later changes have a 30-day cooldown.

I lost my streak! Streaks count calendar nights with a 4am cutoff, and a 🧊 freeze forgives one missed night automatically. If something still looks wrong, email us — we take streak justice seriously.

How do I stop the notifications? iOS Settings → Notifications → Carted, or just never opt in. The ghost only ever sends one "last call" a night, and never on a night you've already played.

How do I delete my data? Everything personal lives on your device — deleting the app deletes it. Details in the privacy policy.
